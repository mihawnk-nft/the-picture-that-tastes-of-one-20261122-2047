A stack of four golden pancakes drowned in glossy maple syrup, crowned
with a melting pat of butter and a wisp of steam — on a cream plate over a
warm dark table. One ingredient, maple syrup, is the whole taste: you can
see it pooling on top, dripping down the sides, and puddling on the plate.

The image is drawn procedurally with the Python standard library only
(`render.py` in the build directory); `main.py` simply copies the bundled
`entry.png` to the output directory.
