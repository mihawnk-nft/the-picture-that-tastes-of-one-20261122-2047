The picture that tastes of one ingredient: a stack of golden pancakes
drowned in glossy maple syrup, with a melting pat of butter and steam
still rising — one look and you can taste the maple.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered
`entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`. It runs
with no network and writes identical bytes on every run. The image was
drawn at build time by `render.py` (kept in the build directory):
every shape — the pancakes, the syrup pool and drips, the butter, the
plate, the steam — is drawn in code.
